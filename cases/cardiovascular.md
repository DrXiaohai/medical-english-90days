# 心血管英语 · 从血压读数到临床表达

A 52-year-old man attends a routine visit after a high blood pressure reading at a pharmacy. He has no chest pain or breathlessness. His father had hypertension. He smokes and spends much of the day sitting. At the clinic, his blood pressure remains elevated after rest. The clinician arranges repeated measurements and discusses home monitoring. No laboratory or imaging results are available at this visit. The clinician explains that hypertension can be asymptomatic and that a single reading does not describe the entire pattern. The patient is advised to return for assessment and to discuss modifiable cardiovascular risk factors.

## Questions

What is the main reason for this visit?

Severe chest pain / A high blood pressure reading / Hemoptysis / Loss of consciousness

Which statement about arteries is correct?

They always carry oxygen-rich blood / They carry blood toward the heart / They carry blood away from the heart / They have no pressure

Which factor in this case is modifiable?

Age / Family history / Sex / Smoking

Why are repeated measurements arranged?

To characterize the blood pressure pattern / To confirm myocardial infarction / To exclude all heart disease / To measure oxygen saturation

Which sentence preserves an association without asserting causation?

Hypertension always causes stroke / Every patient develops stroke / Hypertension is associated with an increased risk of stroke / Stroke proves hypertension

## Answer explanations

1. B. 病例首句给出就诊原因为血压读数升高，其余症状未出现。

2. C. 按离心方向定义动脉，肺动脉是不能用含氧量定义的典型例子。

3. D. 吸烟可干预；年龄、性别、家族史不能通过行为改变。

4. A. 重复测量帮助了解血压模式，不能等同于排除所有心脏病。

5. C. associated with 描述关联；其余为绝对化或不成立的推断。
