# 系统课程临床短病例

## Day 22 · 心血管英语 · 从血压读数到临床表达

A 52-year-old man attends a routine visit after a high blood pressure reading at a pharmacy. He has no chest pain or breathlessness. His father had hypertension. He smokes and spends much of the day sitting. At the clinic, his blood pressure remains elevated after rest. The clinician arranges repeated measurements and discusses home monitoring. No laboratory or imaging results are available at this visit. The clinician explains that hypertension can be asymptomatic and that a single reading does not describe the entire pattern. The patient is advised to return for assessment and to discuss modifiable cardiovascular risk factors.

---

## Day 24 · 心绞痛与劳力性胸痛

A man describes pressure in the center of his chest when walking uphill. It usually subsides after he stops, but the episodes have become more frequent this week. No test results are supplied. The clinician takes the change seriously and arranges prompt assessment. The trainee writes that the history is suggestive of myocardial ischemia while avoiding a claim that a coronary obstruction has already been demonstrated.

---

## Day 25 · 心肌损伤与心肌梗死的措辞

A patient with acute chest discomfort has an elevated troponin result. The clinical team reviews the timing of symptoms, serial measurements, and the electrocardiogram. A student writes that any elevated troponin proves myocardial infarction. The supervising physician corrects the statement: the result indicates myocardial injury, while the diagnosis of infarction requires interpretation in the clinical setting. The supplied information does not include the final diagnosis or the underlying mechanism.

---

## Day 26 · 心力衰竭的症状与超声描述

A woman reports breathlessness when lying flat and increasing ankle swelling. An echocardiogram reports a preserved left ventricular ejection fraction. The trainee concludes that heart failure is impossible. The clinician explains that ejection fraction is one component of assessment and reviews other clinical and echocardiographic findings. The case does not supply enough information for a final classification. The note therefore records symptoms and test findings without using the preserved fraction as a stand-alone exclusion.

---

## Day 27 · 心悸与心房颤动

A man reports episodes of a racing heartbeat. His pulse is irregular during the visit, and an electrocardiogram documents atrial fibrillation. The clinician discusses further assessment, including factors relevant to stroke prevention. A trainee asks whether every patient with palpitations should receive an anticoagulant. The answer is no: the symptom alone neither establishes atrial fibrillation nor determines the balance between expected benefit and bleeding risk.

---

## Day 28 · 心血管周复习：三种胸部表现

Three patients attend different appointments. The first describes chest pressure during exertion, the second feels intermittent rapid beats, and the third becomes breathless when lying flat. No investigation results are provided. A learner matches these descriptions with possible ischemic, rhythm, and congestion-related problems. She labels the matches as working possibilities rather than final diagnoses and writes a separate question for further assessment in each case.

---

## Day 29 · 瓣膜狭窄、反流与杂音

A murmur is heard during a routine examination. Echocardiography later describes valve regurgitation, but the excerpt does not provide its severity or the effect on ventricular function. The patient asks whether the word regurgitation means immediate surgery. The clinician explains that management depends on the complete assessment. The student records the backward-flow concept and avoids translating the finding as severe valve failure when severity has not been stated.

---

## Day 31 · 晕厥与证据不足的病例总结

A man briefly loses consciousness while standing and recovers spontaneously. He recalls palpitations shortly before the event. The electrocardiogram obtained later shows no arrhythmia at that moment. The clinician reviews witnesses, medications, and the sequence of symptoms. The trainee is asked to avoid the sentence cardiac causes have been excluded. The later recording did not capture the event itself, and additional assessment may be required depending on the overall findings.

---

## Day 32 · 呼吸英语 · 阅读一例肺炎病例

A 36-year-old woman presents with three days of fever, cough, and right-sided chest pain that worsens on inspiration. She has no known chronic lung disease and has not recently been hospitalized. On examination, her temperature is 38.4 °C and her respiratory rate is 24 breaths per minute. Crackles are heard at the right lung base. A chest radiograph shows a new right lower-lobe opacity. No microbiological test results are yet available. The clinical picture is consistent with community-acquired pneumonia. The clinician explains that the radiograph supports the assessment but does not identify the pathogen. Further assessment is needed to determine severity and appropriate management.

---

## Day 33 · 哮喘与可变气流受限

A student with episodic wheeze and cough reports that symptoms vary from day to day. An inhaler has been prescribed, but she has difficulty coordinating its use. The clinician reviews the symptom pattern, arranges objective assessment, and checks inhaler technique. The trainee learns to distinguish a report of poor symptom control from proof that the medicine itself is ineffective. No specific dose or final diagnostic test result is supplied.

---

## Day 34 · COPD与持续气流受限

A former smoker reports a long history of cough and gradually increasing exertional breathlessness. He also worked in a dusty environment. The clinician requests spirometry and reviews the exposure history. The trainee initially labels the diagnosis solely from the smoking history. The supervisor explains that exposure raises suspicion but does not replace objective assessment. The case does not provide lung function values, so the final classification and severity remain unspecified.

---

## Day 36 · 肺栓塞的可能性与风险线索

A woman develops sudden breathlessness and pain that worsens with inspiration after a period of reduced mobility. The clinician considers pulmonary embolism among the possibilities and arranges urgent evaluation. The case supplies no diagnostic imaging or laboratory result. The trainee writes a summary that includes onset, the nature of pain, and the risk context, while making clear that the suspected diagnosis has not yet been confirmed.

---

## Day 37 · 通气、氧合与呼吸衰竭

A patient receiving supplemental oxygen has a reassuring oxygen saturation reading but remains drowsy. The clinician does not assume that ventilation is adequate. An arterial blood gas is obtained to assess carbon dioxide and acid-base status as well as oxygenation. The trainee learns that an oxygen saturation value answers a narrower question than a complete assessment of gas exchange. The passage does not provide the blood gas results.

---

## Day 38 · 睡眠呼吸暂停与症状筛查

A partner reports loud snoring and pauses in breathing during sleep. The patient describes daytime sleepiness but also works irregular hours. The clinician records both features and arranges sleep assessment. A questionnaire suggests increased likelihood of obstructive sleep apnea, yet no sleep study result is available. The trainee keeps the screening result separate from a confirmed diagnosis and avoids attributing every episode of sleepiness to a single cause.

---

## Day 40 · 反流、烧心与吞咽症状

A woman reports burning behind the sternum after meals. Over several weeks she has also noticed difficulty swallowing solid food. The clinician records these as separate symptoms and asks about weight loss and bleeding. A trainee translates dysphagia as painful swallowing. The supervisor corrects the wording and explains that difficulty and pain on swallowing can coexist but are not the same complaint. The case does not provide an endoscopic diagnosis.

---

## Day 41 · 消化性溃疡与用药史

A man with recurrent upper abdominal discomfort regularly buys an anti-inflammatory pain medicine without a prescription. He has not previously been tested for Helicobacter pylori. The clinician records both the drug exposure and the missing infection information. The trainee is asked not to describe the pain medicine as a proven cause of an ulcer before evaluation. The discussion focuses on how exposure history guides assessment rather than replacing it.

---

## Day 43 · 胃肠道出血的表现与部位

A patient presents with vomiting of blood and feels faint. The pulse is rapid, and the clinical team begins urgent assessment of circulation while seeking the bleeding source. The trainee records hematemesis and avoids naming an ulcer before investigation. A teaching discussion notes that stool color can suggest a source but is not an infallible anatomical rule. The immediate description must include both the bleeding manifestation and the patient's physiological state.

---

## Day 44 · 肝炎、酶升高与肝功能

A routine blood test reveals elevated aminotransferases. The patient has no visible jaundice. The clinician reviews medicines, supplements, alcohol exposure, and possible infections. A student writes normal liver function because jaundice is absent. The supervisor explains that the absence of jaundice does not cancel the laboratory abnormality and that injury markers differ from measures related to synthetic function. No specific cause is established in the supplied record.

---

## Day 45 · 肝硬化与门静脉高压

A patient with known cirrhosis develops increasing abdominal distension. The record also mentions esophageal varices found previously. The clinician considers fluid accumulation and reviews the current clinical state. The student is asked to connect portal hypertension with possible complications without assuming that every new symptom is explained by the old diagnosis. No active bleeding or change in consciousness is described in the passage.

---

## Day 46 · 腹水分析与新发意识改变

A man with cirrhosis and ascites develops abdominal discomfort and new confusion. The team obtains ascitic fluid for analysis and reviews possible infectious, metabolic, and medication-related contributors. A trainee labels the confusion as hepatic encephalopathy before considering alternatives. The supervisor asks for a broader assessment. The fluid results have not yet returned, so the note describes the concern and investigations rather than naming an organism or a confirmed infection.

---

## Day 47 · 胰腺炎与上腹痛描述

A woman presents with persistent upper abdominal pain radiating to the back and repeated vomiting. Her lipase is elevated, and the team reviews the full clinical picture. An ultrasound is planned to assess the biliary system because she has a history of gallstones. The trainee separates the suspected inflammatory condition from its possible biliary cause. The passage does not provide the imaging result or a final severity assessment.

---

## Day 48 · 炎症性肠病与功能性症状

A young adult has persistent diarrhea, abdominal discomfort, and unintentional weight loss. The clinician arranges investigations rather than labeling the symptoms as irritable bowel syndrome from the history alone. The teaching discussion compares inflammatory bowel disease with a disorder defined by a different clinical framework. No endoscopic or histological results are yet available. The learner summarizes why the symptoms warrant assessment while avoiding a premature distinction between Crohn disease and ulcerative colitis.

---

## Day 49 · 消化系统综合病例复盘

A patient with cirrhosis reports black stools and increasing lightheadedness. The note contains a history of varices but no current endoscopy. A trainee attributes the bleeding to varices with certainty. During review, the clinician explains that the history is relevant while the present source still requires evaluation. The rewritten note records possible gastrointestinal bleeding and the associated symptoms, distinguishes previous findings from current evidence, and avoids postponing assessment until a definitive source is named.

---

## Day 51 · 急性肾损伤与基线比较

A hospitalized patient develops reduced urine output after several days of vomiting. The current creatinine is higher than a result recorded one week earlier. The clinician reviews volume status, medicines, and possible obstruction. A student attributes the change only to dehydration before the assessment is complete. The supervisor notes that the recent rise supports an acute change, while its contributors still require evaluation. No single mechanism is confirmed in the excerpt.

---

## Day 52 · 慢性肾病与持续性证据

A patient is referred after a single low estimated glomerular filtration rate. Earlier records are unavailable, and urine albumin has not been assessed. The clinician requests prior results and plans reassessment. The trainee learns to avoid writing stable chronic kidney disease from this single measurement. The note distinguishes the present estimate, the missing evidence of duration, and the additional information needed to characterize kidney risk.

---

## Day 53 · 蛋白尿与肾病综合征

A patient has leg swelling, low serum albumin, and substantial urinary protein loss. The clinician considers a nephrotic presentation and investigates the underlying renal disease. The trainee is reminded that a syndrome is not the same as a specific histological diagnosis. The passage contains no kidney biopsy result. The summary therefore states the pattern of findings and the need to determine its cause without assigning a particular glomerular disorder.

---

## Day 54 · 尿路症状、感染与梗阻

A woman reports painful, frequent urination with small volumes each time. She has no recorded flank pain or fever in the supplied note, but those features were not explicitly asked about. The clinician clarifies the history and obtains a urine specimen. A trainee writes polyuria and pyelonephritis. The supervisor corrects both overextensions: frequent small voids do not establish high total volume, and the site of infection has not been determined.

---

## Day 55 · 电解质、液体与透析表述

A patient with worsening kidney function has a high potassium result. The team assesses symptoms and the electrocardiogram while reviewing sample quality and the overall biochemical picture. The trainee asks whether a particular creatinine value automatically requires dialysis. The clinician explains that decisions consider complications and the clinical context. The passage provides neither a numerical threshold nor a complete dialysis indication, so the learner summarizes the factors under assessment.

---

## Day 56 · 泌尿系统周测与因果措辞

A summary lists rising creatinine, frequent small voids, and a new medicine started recently. Earlier kidney results are incomplete. The trainee writes chronic drug-induced kidney failure with polyuria. During review, each part is questioned: chronicity is uncertain, causation is unproven, severity is not fully described, and frequency does not establish total urine volume. The revised summary retains the observed change and explicitly lists the information needed next.

---

## Day 57 · 血细胞与全血细胞计数

A complete blood count shows reduced hemoglobin with a platelet count within the laboratory reference interval. The total white cell count is also within that interval. A blood film is requested to examine red cell morphology. The report does not describe pancytopenia because all three cell lines are not reduced. The clinician uses the count, cell size, and appearance together when planning the next stage of anemia evaluation.

---

## Day 58 · 缺铁性贫血与失血线索

A patient has low hemoglobin, small red cells, and low ferritin. The findings support iron deficiency. During follow-up, the clinician asks about diet, menstrual history where relevant, and possible gastrointestinal blood loss. The patient had focused only on correcting the laboratory value. The discussion explains why replacing iron without investigating ongoing loss may leave the underlying problem unresolved. No specific bleeding source is established in the case.

---

## Day 59 · 大细胞性贫血与B12

A patient with macrocytic anemia describes tingling in both feet. Vitamin B12 is low, and further history reveals a condition affecting absorption. The team considers the blood and neurological findings together. The explanation distinguishes the role of B12 from a general label of poor diet. The patient understands that the laboratory abnormality may be linked to absorption even when food intake appears adequate, and that follow-up should address more than hemoglobin alone.

---

## Day 60 · 溶血与网织红细胞反应

A patient with anemia has increased reticulocytes and indirect bilirubin, with reduced haptoglobin. The clinician interprets the combination as supporting increased red cell destruction. A peripheral smear is reviewed to seek additional clues. The results describe a process, not its complete cause. The patient is told that further testing will depend on the clinical history and cell morphology rather than on a single marker in isolation.

---

## Day 61 · 血小板与凝血异常

A patient notices small nonblanching spots on the legs and gum bleeding. The platelet count is low. The laboratory reviews the sample for platelet clumping, and the clinician asks about medicines and recent illness. The patient has no information about coagulation factor tests in the supplied record. The assessment therefore considers a platelet-related problem while avoiding the claim that every component of coagulation has been measured.

---

## Day 62 · 白血病、原始细胞与骨髓

A patient presents with fatigue, recurrent infections, and easy bruising. The blood count shows abnormalities in several cell lines, and the smear contains immature cells requiring further characterization. Hematology assessment is arranged, including evaluation of the marrow where appropriate. The family asks whether the total white cell count alone can determine the subtype. The clinician explains that morphology and additional laboratory characterization are needed for classification.

---

## Day 63 · 血液周复习：形态、数量与病因

Two patients have similar hemoglobin concentrations. One has small red cells and low ferritin; the other has increased reticulocytes and biochemical evidence supporting hemolysis. Their summaries should not be identical simply because the hemoglobin values are close. The first pattern points toward iron deficiency, while the second suggests accelerated destruction. Both still require assessment of the underlying cause. The exercise asks the learner to explain the reasoning in two concise English paragraphs.

---

## Day 64 · 淋巴结与持续性肿大

A patient notices a cervical lump that has persisted beyond the resolution of a recent sore throat. The clinician examines other nodal regions and asks about weight change and systemic symptoms. The lump is documented as lymphadenopathy pending further assessment. The patient asks whether persistence means lymphoma. The explanation acknowledges that persistence matters but does not assign a malignant cause from that feature alone. The next steps will depend on the complete examination and history.

---

## Day 65 · 免疫识别与抗体检测

A person receives a positive antibody result during screening. The report names the target but does not include the relevant exposure history or symptom timing. The clinician explains that the meaning depends on what the assay detects and when it was performed. The person initially interprets any positive antibody as proof of an active infection. The discussion separates immune recognition from the narrower claim that a current symptomatic infection has been established.

---

## Day 66 · 过敏反应的范围与紧迫性

Shortly after an exposure, a patient develops widespread wheals, wheeze, and lightheadedness. The clinical team treats the situation as an emergency and evaluates airway, breathing, and circulation. The record does not describe an isolated skin complaint. Later documentation distinguishes the observed reaction from the still-investigated identity of the trigger. A complete allergy history includes the substance, timing, manifestations, and treatment rather than the single word allergy.

---

## Day 67 · 系统性红斑狼疮的多系统信息

A young woman reports joint pain and a rash after sunlight exposure. Urine testing also shows protein. An autoantibody test is positive. The clinician considers systemic autoimmune disease and evaluates organ involvement. The available findings do not specify a final diagnosis or the renal pathology. The case illustrates why a positive antibody should be integrated with clinical manifestations rather than used as a stand-alone label for every future symptom.

---

## Day 68 · 免疫抑制与感染风险沟通

A patient receiving immunosuppressive treatment feels well and asks whether follow-up can stop. The clinician explains that symptom improvement does not remove the need to monitor treatment-related risks. The visit includes a review of infections, blood counts, and vaccination history. Recommendations about vaccines are individualized according to immune status and vaccine type. The patient is advised to report new symptoms promptly rather than assuming that remission means the absence of every possible complication.

---

## Day 70 · 免疫与内分泌衔接复习

A patient brings two reports: an antibody result and a hormone panel. She feels better than at the previous visit but asks whether both disorders have been excluded. The clinician explains that symptom improvement, immune markers, and hormone measurements answer different questions. The results must be interpreted in their own clinical contexts. The exercise asks the learner to write one sentence about what each report can contribute and another about what remains unresolved.

---

## Day 71 · 糖尿病、血糖与长期暴露

A patient with known diabetes brings home glucose readings and a glycated hemoglobin result. The clinician reviews the daily pattern rather than treating a single favorable reading as proof of sustained control. Recent anemia is also noted because red cell-related factors may influence interpretation of glycated hemoglobin. The visit includes discussion of kidney monitoring and other complications. The patient is encouraged to describe barriers to the agreed treatment plan so that follow-up can address practical difficulties.

---

## Day 72 · 低血糖与症状时间关联

A person using insulin becomes sweaty and confused after missing a meal. A glucose measurement obtained during the symptoms is low, and the symptoms improve after glucose is corrected under clinical supervision. At review, the clinician examines meal timing, insulin use, activity, and kidney function. A satisfactory long-term average does not erase this episode. The follow-up plan focuses on understanding the circumstances and reducing the chance of recurrence.

---

## Day 73 · 酮症酸中毒的机制与监测

A patient with diabetes presents with thirst, frequent urination, vomiting, and deep breathing. The laboratory reports ketones and metabolic acidosis. The clinical team evaluates circulation and electrolytes while managing the acute illness. Although the initial potassium concentration is not low, the clinician explains that body potassium balance can differ from the measured concentration and can change during treatment. Repeated monitoring is therefore part of the management rather than a sign that the first test was unnecessary.

---

## Day 74 · 甲状腺功能与症状对照

Two patients describe opposite temperature preferences: one feels unusually hot and has tremor, while the other feels cold and reports constipation. Thyroid tests are requested for each. The clinician explains that the symptoms provide clues but cannot independently establish thyroid function. The interpretation will consider the stimulating hormone together with thyroid hormone measurements. Medication use and the clinical setting are reviewed before a conclusion is made.

---

## Day 75 · 肾上腺激素与应激

A patient who has used a glucocorticoid for a prolonged period becomes unwell during an intercurrent illness. The clinician asks how the medicine was taken and whether it was recently stopped. Symptoms include weakness and low blood pressure. The assessment considers impaired stress response alongside other causes of acute illness. The record does not give a cortisol result or a tapering schedule, so the learner must not invent a specific withdrawal regimen.

---

## Day 77 · 内分泌与处方周复习

A patient reports taking the medicine exactly as written on an old discharge sheet, although the dose was changed at a later visit. The current list and the old sheet therefore conflict. Recent symptoms prompt a review of actual use rather than an assumption of intentional nonadherence. The team reconciles the documents and checks understanding. The exercise asks the learner to distinguish the prescribed plan, the instructions available to the patient, and what was actually taken.

---

## Day 79 · 抗凝监测与风险沟通

A patient receiving an anticoagulant starts a new medicine from another clinic. At follow-up, the team checks for interactions, changes in kidney function, and bleeding symptoms. The patient assumes that the laboratory monitoring used by a relative applies to every anticoagulant. The clinician explains that monitoring requirements are drug-specific. The review aims to preserve the expected benefit while identifying preventable harm, and it does not provide a universal target value for all agents.

---

## Day 80 · 利尿药、容量与电解质

A patient treated for congestion reports less breathlessness and a decrease in ankle swelling. Weight has fallen, but dizziness on standing has appeared. The team reviews blood pressure, kidney function, and electrolytes before deciding whether the current plan remains appropriate. The account contains both improvement and a possible treatment-related concern. The learner is asked to retain both in the English summary rather than describe the response as uniformly favorable.

---

## Day 83 · 依从性访谈与共同决策

A patient takes fewer doses than prescribed because the medicine causes symptoms that interfere with work. At first, the chart describes nonadherence without explanation. During a discussion, the clinician asks what happens after each dose and which barriers matter most. The patient and clinician review benefits, harms, and feasible alternatives. The revised note records the actual pattern and the reason for it, allowing follow-up to assess whether the agreed plan is workable.

---

## Day 85 · 卒中病例与最后正常时间

A patient is found with new right-sided weakness and difficulty producing meaningful language on waking. A family member last saw the patient well before sleep. The emergency team documents both the discovery time and the last known well time and obtains urgent brain imaging. The learner avoids treating the waking time as a proven onset time. The case focuses on accurate chronology and symptom description while the cause and treatment eligibility are assessed.

---

## Day 86 · 肿瘤分期与病理分级

A biopsy establishes carcinoma in a sampled lesion. The pathology report includes a grade, while imaging to assess disease extent is still being arranged. The patient asks whether the grade tells how far the cancer has spread. The clinician explains that grade and stage answer different questions. The learner writes a summary that records the tissue diagnosis and keeps the extent of disease pending until the relevant assessment is available.

---

## Day 87 · 影像报告与检验前概率

Imaging requested for one complaint reveals a small unrelated abnormality. The report recommends correlation with clinical information rather than assigning a diagnosis from appearance alone. The patient interprets the finding as definite cancer. The clinician explains the concept of an incidental finding and reviews the reason for the original test, the likelihood of disease before testing, and the limits of the image. Further action depends on the specific features and context.

---

## Day 88 · 多病共存与跨系统交接

A patient with heart failure, kidney disease, and diabetes is preparing for discharge. Breathlessness has improved, but kidney function and glucose patterns require continued review. The team documents the latest findings, reconciles medicines, and names the pending follow-up tasks. A relative asks which problems are resolved. The answer distinguishes symptom improvement from ongoing monitoring needs. The written handover identifies who will review the pending results and avoids implying that all chronic risks have disappeared.
