# 呼吸英语 · 阅读一例肺炎病例

A 36-year-old woman presents with three days of fever, cough, and right-sided chest pain that worsens on inspiration. She has no known chronic lung disease and has not recently been hospitalized. On examination, her temperature is 38.4 °C and her respiratory rate is 24 breaths per minute. Crackles are heard at the right lung base. A chest radiograph shows a new right lower-lobe opacity. No microbiological test results are yet available. The clinical picture is consistent with community-acquired pneumonia. The clinician explains that the radiograph supports the assessment but does not identify the pathogen. Further assessment is needed to determine severity and appropriate management.

## Questions

Which term describes subjective breathing discomfort?

Hypoxemia / Consolidation / Dyspnea / Sputum

Which finding is reported on imaging?

A normal radiograph / A new right lower-lobe opacity / A left pleural mass / Bilateral rib fractures

Which is the best-supported preliminary diagnosis among these options?

Isolated gastritis / Uncomplicated cystitis / Hypothyroidism / Community-acquired pneumonia

What remains unknown in the passage?

The pathogen / The duration of symptoms / The location of crackles / The respiratory rate

What does “pain that worsens on inspiration” mean?

Pain disappears with breathing / Pain occurs only after eating / Pain becomes worse when breathing in / Pain proves myocardial infarction

## Answer explanations

1. C. dyspnea 表示主观不适；hypoxemia 是血氧异常，不能互换。

2. B. 病例明确右下肺新出现阴影，未描述其他选项。

3. D. 发热、咳嗽、局灶啰音和新阴影综合支持社区获得性肺炎；未给出足够信息判断病原体。

4. A. 无微生物学结果，不能凭影像确定病原体；其余均已给出。

5. C. that 从句限定 pain；on inspiration 表示吸气时，不表示病因确诊。
